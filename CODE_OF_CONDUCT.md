# Community guidelines

Be respectful, specific and patient. Welcome beginners and explain feedback in ways people can act on. Critique ideas without attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Share only media and data you have permission to publish. Do not identify people seen by a camera in issue reports. Keep discussion relevant to learning, implementation and responsible operation of FlowCount.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict participation. For a private concern, use the contact channel on [DeAI Labs](https://deai.science/). Do not include someone else's sensitive information in a public report.
