# Commercial licensing

FlowCount's original code is available at no charge for the permitted purposes
in the **[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE)**. For commercial uses
outside those purposes, obtain a **separate paid written license from DeAI Labs
before starting that use**.

This document explains the commercial route. It is not itself a commercial
license, quote, automatic royalty schedule or permission to start a deployment.

## When to contact DeAI Labs

Examples that normally need a commercial agreement, unless already covered by
the standard license's permitted purposes, include:

- Selling FlowCount-based counters, firmware or packaged products.
- Charging for installations, integrations, customization or managed counting.
- Embedding the software in a paid product, service or commercial platform.
- Operating it for a commercial business's production analytics or operations.
- Commercial redistribution or licensing of a derivative implementation.

The standard license also explicitly permits uses by certain educational,
charitable, public research, health, environmental and government institutions.
Those express permissions remain intact. Read [the licensing guide](docs/LICENSING.md)
and the binding license; the examples above do not override its text.

## Request an agreement

Use the contact channel at **[deai.science](https://deai.science/)** and include:

1. Your organization and intended product/service or internal use.
2. Expected number of devices, sites and customers.
3. Whether you will modify or redistribute firmware or source.
4. Expected support, integration and deployment requirements.

DeAI Labs will discuss scope, price, term, distribution and support in a separate
agreement. **No fee, revenue percentage or usage threshold is set by this
repository.** A payment by itself is not a substitute for an agreed license.

DeAI Labs can license only rights it holds or is authorized to license.
Third-party dependencies and models retain their terms; a commercial agreement
does not waive those obligations. Contributions are accepted only after their
licensing rights are agreed with their authors.
