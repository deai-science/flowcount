# Licensing explained

FlowCount uses a **source-available, noncommercial licensing model**, with a
separate paid commercial route. This is not an OSI-approved open-source release
and it is not AGPL, GPL or MIT.

## What is free, and what requires an agreement?

| Situation | Route |
| --- | --- |
| Personal study, hobby use and noncommercial experimentation without anticipated commercial application | Free under LICENSE |
| Noncommercial use, modification and redistribution complying with notices | Free under LICENSE |
| Uses by the organizations expressly listed in the standard license's Noncommercial Organizations section | Permitted under LICENSE, regardless of their funding source or funding obligations |
| Commercial uses outside the license's permitted purposes, including ordinary sales, paid services and business production deployment | Separate paid written agreement with DeAI Labs before use |
| Use of third-party components or models | Their own licenses also apply |

The organizational permission includes charitable organizations, educational
institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health
organizations, environmental protection organizations and government
institutions. This is an express provision of the unmodified standard license;
it must not be silently replaced by a broader claim that everyone who receives
funding owes a royalty.

The binding text is [LICENSE](../LICENSE). Keep its terms or URL and the
`Required Notice:` line in [NOTICE](../NOTICE) when distributing copies.
Publishing modifications does not automatically authorize a commercial use.
The commercial route and contact process are in
[COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](../COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md).

## Why this license?

The project owner requested free community/noncommercial use and a paid route
for commercial exploitation. The **unmodified PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0**
provides an established noncommercial software license rather than an invented
"MIT plus payment" clause. DeAI Labs offers a separate agreement for rights it
controls; no price or automatic percentage is invented here.

[Vitalik Buterin's July 2025 article](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2025/07/07/copyleft.html)
discusses copyleft and sharing derivative source. Copyleft does not by itself
require a payment because someone earns money. FlowCount's chosen noncommercial
model is different and is not presented as a license written or endorsed by
Vitalik. The [OSI definition](https://opensource.org/faq) permits commercial use,
which is why this repository describes itself as source-available.

Official text: [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0).

## Scope and third-party rights

The project license covers original FlowCount software and documentation to the
extent rights are held by the project. Existing upstream notices are preserved:

- IMX219 ISP tuning: Apache-2.0, with `config/LICENSE`.
- Captive DNS example: Unlicense OR CC0-1.0, as stated in its source headers.
- Downloaded ESP-IDF components and model: their own licenses and notices.
- Names/logos and hardware marks: no trademark endorsement or hardware-design
  license is granted by the software license.

Read [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and [media provenance](MEDIA.md).
Do not replace upstream licenses with the FlowCount license. A source release
does not redistribute downloaded components or model/firmware binaries.

The earlier local MIT draft was never the published code release: the remote
repository initially contained only a short placeholder README without code or
license. This release uses the terms selected above. If anyone received a copy
under a prior valid license, this document does not purport to revoke those
already-granted rights.

## Contributions

Authors retain rights in their contributions. DeAI Labs must have appropriate
permission before offering contributed code commercially. Discuss licensing
before sending code, and complete any necessary contributor agreement before
merge. A Developer Certificate of Origin alone is not a commercial relicensing
grant.
